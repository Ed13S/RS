Page 1: Topic 1 — Squares & Squaring

Q1. Evaluate integer square: 162
• 16 × 16
Answer: 256
Q2. Evaluate integer square: 252
• 25 × 25
Answer: 625
Q3. Evaluate with brackets: (-11)2
• (-11) × (-11)
• Negative base inside brackets gives a positive result.
Answer: 121
Q4. Evaluate without brackets: -122
• -(12 × 12)
• The negative sign applies after squaring.
Answer: -144
Q5. Evaluate decimal square: (0.4)2
• 42 = 16
• Base has 1 decimal place, square must have 2 decimal places.
Answer: 0.16
Q6. Evaluate decimal square: (0.12)2
• 122 = 144
• Base has 2 decimal places, square must have 4 decimal places.
Answer: 0.0144
Q7. Evaluate fraction square: (5/9)2
• 52 / 92
Answer: 25/81
Q8. Evaluate mixed fraction square: (1 1/3)2
• Convert mixed fraction to improper: 1 1/3 = 4/3
• (4/3)2 = 42 / 32
Answer: 16/9 (or 1 7/9)

Page 2: Topic 2 — Square Roots & Radicals

Q1. Evaluate square root: √289
• Find x where x × x = 289
Answer: 17
Q2. Evaluate square root: √400
• √4 × √100 = 2 × 10
Answer: 20
Q3. Evaluate fraction root: √(64/225)
• √64 / √225
Answer: 8/15
Q4. Evaluate decimal root: √(0.0144)
• √144 = 12
• Radicand has 4 decimal places, root has 2.
Answer: 0.12
Q5. Simplify expression: √81 × √25
• Evaluate terms: 9 × 5
Answer: 45
Q6. Simplify combined expression: √(9 + 16) + √144
• Under radical first: √(25) = 5
• Second term: √144 = 12
• Combine: 5 + 12
Answer: 17
Q7. Estimate to nearest integer: √75
• Perfect squares around 75: 64 (82) and 81 (92)
• 75 is closer to 81 than 64.
Answer: 9
Q8. Solve equation for x: x2 = 225
• x = ±√225
Answer: x = ±15

Page 3: Topic 3 — Index Laws 1 & 2 (Product & Quotient)

Q1. Simplify: x5 × x8
• Apply Product Rule: Add powers (5 + 8)
Answer: x13
Q2. Simplify: m12 ÷ m4
• Apply Quotient Rule: Subtract powers (12 - 4)
Answer: m8
Q3. Simplify with coefficients: 4a3 × 6a5
• Multiply coefficients: 4 × 6 = 24
• Add powers for base 'a': 3 + 5 = 8
Answer: 24a8
Q4. Simplify with coefficients: 36p9 ÷ 9p5
• Divide coefficients: 36 ÷ 9 = 4
• Subtract powers for base 'p': 9 - 5 = 4
Answer: 4p4
Q5. Multiple variables: 2a4b2 × 5a3b6
• Coefficients: 2 × 5 = 10
• Base 'a': a4+3 = a7
• Base 'b': b2+6 = b8
Answer: 10a7b8
Q6. Multiple variables: (42x7y10) / (7x4y2)
• Coefficients: 42 ÷ 7 = 6
• Base 'x': x7-4 = x3
• Base 'y': y10-2 = y8
Answer: 6x3y8
Q7. Combined operations: (x3 × x7) / x4
• Numerator: x3+7 = x10
• Divide by denominator: x10-4
Answer: x6
Q8. Combined operations: (12m8 × 2m3) / (6m5)
• Numerator: (12 × 2)m8+3 = 24m11
• Divide: (24/6)m11-5
Answer: 4m6

Page 4: Topic 4 — Index Laws 3 & 4 (Powers & Brackets)

Q1. Simplify power of power: (m3)2
• Apply Power Law: Multiply powers (3 × 2)
Answer: m6
Q2. Expand bracket: (2x4)3
• Distribute power: 23 × (x4)3
• Evaluate: 8 × x12
Answer: 8x12
Q3. Expand bracket: (5a2b5)2
• Distribute power: 52 × (a2)2 × (b5)2
• Evaluate: 25 × a4 × b10
Answer: 25a4b10
Q4. Power of quotient: (x3 / 3)4
• Numerator: (x3)4 = x12
• Denominator: 34 = 81
Answer: x12 / 81
Q5. Combined power expression: (a3)2 × a4
• Power law first: a6 × a4
• Product law: a6+4
Answer: a10
Q6. Combined expression: (2y3)4 ÷ 8y5
• Expand numerator: 24 × (y3)4 = 16y12
• Divide: (16/8) × y12-5
Answer: 2y7
Q7. Complex brackets: ((p2)3)4
• Multiply all exponents: 2 × 3 × 4
Answer: p24
Q8. Simplify fractional form: (3a4b2)3 / (9a5b)
• Expand numerator: 33a12b6 = 27a12b6
• Divide coefficients: 27 / 9 = 3
• Subtract powers: a12-5 = a7, b6-1 = b5
Answer: 3a7b5
